FeatureIcon is the small circle-icon + label used in a row of 3 under the hero headline to call out product benefits.

```jsx
<FeatureIcon icon="assets/icons/nav/beneficios.webp" label="MÁS SUAVIDAD<br>PARA TU PIEL" />
```

Always pair 3 across in `.hero-icons` — skin, hair/frizz, 100% mulberry silk are the canonical three.
