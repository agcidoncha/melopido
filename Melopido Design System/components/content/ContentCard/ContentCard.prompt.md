ContentCard links to a guide/SEO article (benefits, care, colors, sizes) — used in 2-column grids on content pages.

```jsx
<ContentCard title="Beneficios" href="beneficios-funda-almohada-seda.html">
  Descubre cómo la seda de morera cuida tu piel y tu cabello cada noche.
</ContentCard>
```

Hovers lift 4px with a soft shadow (see styles.css `.content-card:hover`).
